# Records and decision examples

Use these only where they clarify the assignment; they are not mandatory extra files or fixed report headings.

## Compact records

Original item: `id | claim/option | evidence or premise | confidence/limits | bears on`.

Reconciliation: `original IDs | relationship | final destination/disposition | reason | consequential unresolved issue`.

Review: `reviewer | report hash | representation OK or concrete defect | substantive reservation if material | changed sections rechecked`.

An alternative can be an expanded option list, a conditional recommendation, or a short explanation of unresolved interpretation. When a comparison helps, use: `path | when preferable | evidence/assumptions | benefit and cost/risk | what would favor it`. There need not be exactly two positions or a named agent on every row.

## Worked examples

**Agreement plus a complement.** Two peers independently find the same two limitations. One adds a supported third condition. Integrate all three, credit discovery in provenance, and omit a dissent section. Peer agreement does not establish that their shared database was complete.

**Supported minority evidence.** Two peers favor an in-memory queue. A third documents restart loss under the user's durability requirement. Investigate that constraint and revise the recommendation if the evidence warrants it. Two votes cannot override the requirement; the final answer may favor the minority discovery without becoming a debate transcript.

**Different operating assumptions.** One legal analysis concerns attorney-reviewed internal drafting; another concerns an unsupervised client-facing service. Explain the conditional conclusions under those facts. If the actual operating model is unknown, identify that uncertainty. Do not manufacture disagreement over a single fact pattern or require identical searches before reporting the distinction. This is a workflow example, not a legal conclusion.

**A corrected original.** An original calls a rule mandatory, but the authoritative text makes it permissive. Correct the claim and record its disposition. Preserve any surviving useful insight. The false mandatory reading need not remain as a dissent.

**Valid reservation versus report defect.** A reviewer accepts the report's trade-off analysis but prefers option B; the report can be complete with that material alternative. If the summary falsely says all peers prefer A, repair it. Quoting the reviewer in an appendix does not fix the summary.

**New value from combination.** One peer proposes staged migration; another proposes a reversible interface shim. Consider a staged reversible migration as new synthesis. Check compatibility, expense, and rollback conditions with a peer. Do not imply both independently proposed that combination.

**Missing final review.** An original exists but its author is unavailable for required representation review. Deliver the useful provisional report and identify the unconfirmed review. Do not call it fully reviewed, erase completed research, or keep debating with the available peer as a substitute.

**An uncertain premise remains.** Credible sources disagree about whether a constraint applies. The report can complete its agreed research scope by accurately presenting the conflict, consequences, and evidence needed to resolve it. Falsely presenting one source as settled would instead be a report defect.
